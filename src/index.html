About

Problems in software organisations are socio-technological. People and technology are intertwined, and improving one side on its own does not work. When AI makes coding cheap, the pressure does not disappear — it moves to the next node along: review, verification, decision-making, budget. Every node has to scale in proportion, and the ones that do not are where a team starts to hurt.

I have spent eleven years across the SaaS value chain — sales and business development, solution engineering, full-stack, requirements and UX, and now engineering management. Today I lead an international engineering team. Before that I was Tech Lead on a seven-figure Audi digitisation project, and a founding engineer at a Karlsruhe startup building a manufacturing execution system that runs in five European factories.

Since 2010 I have put in more than 2,000 hours as a trainer and coach, mentored over twenty juniors and interns, and I coach student startups here in Karlsruhe. I hold an MSc in Information Engineering and Management from KIT.
